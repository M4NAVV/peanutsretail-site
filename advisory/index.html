Questions brands ask

Straight answers.

Do you advise brands that compete with the ones you run?

No. We never advise a direct competitor of a brand we operate, in a category we operate. If there is a conflict, we say so in the first conversation.

Do you only work in the East and North-East?

That is where we know the ground best, and where we lead. We also run stores in Bengaluru, and our store launch and health check work applies wherever you are opening.

What does it cost?

The first conversation is a paid thirty-minute session with our director; we confirm the fee with your time. After that, every engagement has a fixed fee, agreed in a one-page proposal before we start. We do not bill by the hour.

Who actually does the work?

Our director leads every engagement, with the operations team that runs our own stores.

Will our figures stay private?

Yes. We never publish a client's figures, and one client's numbers never appear in another's work.

Can you run the stores after opening them?

Yes. That is our Run it for you offer, and it can become a long-term franchise partnership.
