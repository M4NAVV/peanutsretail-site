Our story

Peanuts Retail opened its first store in Siliguri in the mid-nineties, when organised retail in the region was only beginning. Over three decades the company has grown with the region it serves, across North Bengal, Sikkim, the North-East, Kolkata and Odisha, as a long-term retail partner to some of India's best-known fashion houses.

In April 2026 we brought that experience to Bengaluru with Manyavar and Mohey, our first step into the South.

What sets us apart is how we run retail. We choose sites with care, build every store to its brand's design, train our own teams, and hold each one to the same standard every day. It is why our partners keep growing with us, and why our stores have been recognised by them year after year.
