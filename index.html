The company

A retailer that grew with the cities it serves.

Peanuts opened its first multi-brand store in Siliguri in the mid-nineties, when organised retail in the city was only beginning. The company grew alongside it, adding one brand partnership at a time, and today runs stores from the hills of Sikkim to the high streets of Bengaluru.
